# Walls

Terracotta walls work like cobblestone walls. They connect to other walls, to solid blocks, and to fence gates, glass panes and iron bars. They are 1.5 blocks tall for collision, so mobs cannot jump over them.

## Behavior

- **Connections:** every terracotta wall connects to every other wall, including vanilla walls and walls from other mods that use the `#minecraft:walls` tag. You can mix colors freely.
- **Posts:** a wall shows a taller post at its ends and corners, and where something sits on top of it, like a torch or lantern.
- **Mining:** walls need a pickaxe to drop, and take as long to mine as the full terracotta block.
- **Blast resistance and sounds:** the same as the full block.
- **Glazed walls:** like glazed terracotta, a piston can push them but a sticky piston cannot pull them back.

Every terracotta wall is in the vanilla `#minecraft:walls` tag. See [Tags](../reference/tags).
