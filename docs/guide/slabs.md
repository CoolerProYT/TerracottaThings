# Slabs

Terracotta slabs are half blocks. Place one on the top or bottom half of a block, then place a second slab of the same kind into the same space to make a double slab.

<ShapeRecipe shape="slab" />

## Behavior

Slabs copy the block they are made from:

- **Mining:** they need a pickaxe to drop, and take as long to mine as the full terracotta block.
- **Double slabs** drop two slabs. A double slab looks exactly like the full terracotta block.
- **Blast resistance and sounds:** the same as the full block.
- **Glazed slabs:** like glazed terracotta, a piston can push them but a sticky piston cannot pull them back.

Plain, dyed and glazed slabs are all mineable with a pickaxe. Slabs are in the vanilla `#minecraft:slabs` tag, with one exception listed on the [Tags](../reference/tags) page.
