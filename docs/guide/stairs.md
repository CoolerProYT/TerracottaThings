# Stairs

Terracotta stairs place and connect like any vanilla stairs: they turn into corner pieces next to other stairs, and you can place them upside down by clicking the top half of a block.

<ShapeRecipe shape="stairs" />

## Behavior

Stairs copy the block they are made from:

- **Mining:** they need a pickaxe to drop, and take as long to mine as the full terracotta block.
- **Blast resistance and sounds:** the same as the full block.
- **Glazed stairs:** like glazed terracotta, a piston can push them but a sticky piston cannot pull them back.

Plain, dyed and glazed stairs are all mineable with a pickaxe. Stairs are in the vanilla `#minecraft:stairs` tag, with one exception listed on the [Tags](../reference/tags) page.
