# FAQ

## Do I need a resource pack?

No. Every block uses the vanilla texture of the terracotta it is made from, so it also matches any resource pack that retextures terracotta.

## Does it need to be on the server?

Yes. The blocks are new content, so the server and every player need the mod.

## Why can't I cut these blocks in a stonecutter?

Vanilla has no stonecutter recipes for terracotta, and the mod follows that. A datapack can add them. See [Adding a stonecutter recipe](./reference/recipes#adding-a-stonecutter-recipe).

## Why did my stairs, slab or wall drop nothing?

Like the terracotta they are made from, they only drop when mined with a pickaxe. Buttons and pressure plates drop with any tool.

## Do terracotta walls connect to other walls?

Yes. They connect to every wall in the `#minecraft:walls` tag, including all vanilla walls and the other terracotta walls.

## Why doesn't a sticky piston pull my glazed stairs back?

Glazed terracotta can be pushed but never pulled, and the glazed stairs, slabs and walls keep that behavior.

## Is there a config?

No. Recipes, drops and tags are data files, so you can change them with a datapack. See [For modpack makers](./reference/).
